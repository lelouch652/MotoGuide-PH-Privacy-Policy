01

Local-Only Rider Data Storage

All personal records you enter inside MotoGuide PH remain strictly on your Android device inside a private local Room SQLite database managed by MotoTech. This includes:

  • Traffic citation logs (TOP/OVR ticket numbers, violation codes, issuing agencies, dates, deadlines, fine amounts, demerit points, settlement OR references, and rider notes).
  • Bookmarked LTO guidelines, pre-ride safety checklist completions, and UI customization preferences (theme, accent palette, font scale, card density, and dashboard section layout).

MotoTech does not maintain a remote database of your riding history or citation activity, and none of these entries are transmitted off your device.

02

Citation Ticket Photos & Android Photo Picker

When MotoGuide PH Pro subscribers attach an image of a physical citation ticket or official settlement receipt, the app uses the privacy-preserving Android System Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia).

  • MotoGuide PH never requests broad media or external storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES).
  • Only the single image you explicitly choose is copied into MotoGuide PH’s private app-internal storage directory so you can view it offline.
  • Deleting a citation inside the app immediately deletes its associated local ticket photo file from private app storage.
03

Bundled Official LTO Documents & Offline Search

All 103 official Land Transportation Office (LTO), DOTr, and DENR reference issuances—including Republic Acts, Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRRs), Administrative Orders, Department Orders, Memorandum Circulars, and Office Orders—are packaged directly inside the application.

Global Search queries across guidelines, violation fine schedules, and the 103 official PDFs execute 100% locally on your device. Your searches are never logged to external servers. When you choose to share or export a printable PDF compendium or official document, sharing occurs only through the Android system share sheet using a scoped FileProvider URI that you initiate.

04

MotoGuide PH Pro Subscriptions & Google Play Billing

Optional Monthly (₱99/month) and Yearly (₱799/year with a 7-day free trial) MotoGuide PH Pro subscriptions are processed directly by Google Play Billing (com.android.billingclient).

  • MotoTech never receives, accesses, or stores your credit card numbers, debit card details, GCash/Maya credentials, or Google Pay payment instruments.
  • Google Play communicates only minimal purchase state metadata (selected plan ID, subscription status, renewal/expiry timestamp, and purchase token) to verify and unlock Pro features locally on your device.
  • Authorized Google Play App Review access credentials entered in the app’s reviewer verification dialog are checked locally on-device solely to unlock Pro features for store review testing and are never uploaded externally.
05

Permissions, Analytics & Zero Third-Party Tracking

MotoTech built MotoGuide PH with a strict least-privilege posture:

  • No Advertising SDKs: No third-party ad networks, ad mediation libraries, or behavioral tracking pixels.
  • No Analytics Telemetry: No third-party crash-profiling trackers or behavioral session-recording SDKs.
  • No Sensitive Sensor Harvesting: MotoGuide PH does not collect background GPS location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, microphone audio, or advertising IDs.

07

Your Rights & Data Deletion (RA 10173)

In accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you retain full ownership and control over every record stored on your device:

  • Access & Correction: View or edit any citation log, ticket number, fine, or note directly inside Citation History.
  • Targeted Deletion: Delete individual citation records (which also deletes any attached ticket photo) or reset UI customization settings at any time.
  • Complete Erasure: Clear App Storage in Android System Settings or uninstall MotoGuide PH to permanently erase the local database and all stored images.
  • Subscription Management: Manage or cancel auto-renewal anytime via Google Play Subscriptions or in-app Pro management.

Children’s Privacy & Policy Updates

MotoGuide PH is designed for licensed motorcycle riders and vehicle owners in the Philippines and is not directed to children under the age of 13. MotoTech does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If MotoTech updates this Privacy Policy to reflect new app capabilities or regulatory requirements, the updated policy and effective date will be published at this URL and inside the MotoGuide PH app.
